# EyeFit – Optical Store Management Software
### Proposed Features

A modern web-based software to run all **3 stores** from one place: customers, eye-test records,
billing, and stock, with owner-level visibility across every branch.

Works in any browser on a computer, tablet, or mobile. Nothing to install. Data is stored safely in the cloud.

Legend: ✅ Included in first version  |  🔜 Coming next  |  ⭐ Optional add-on

---

## 1. Multi-Store Management
- ✅ All 3 stores managed from one software, each with its own stock, bills and invoice numbers
- ✅ Owner can switch between stores or see **all stores combined**
- ✅ Staff logins locked to their own store
- ✅ Role-based access: Owner / Manager / Staff (e.g. staff cannot delete bills or see reports)
- 🔜 Transfer stock from one store to another
- 🔜 Compare branch performance side by side

## 2. Customer Database
- ✅ Customer profile: name, 2 mobile numbers, email, gender, DOB/age, address, category, referring doctor, remarks
- ✅ **Instant search by mobile number or name**, finds the customer as you type
- ✅ Complete history on one page: every eye test, every bill, pending balance
- ✅ Customers shared across stores, so a customer who visits any branch is recognised
- 🔜 Import existing customer data from the current software
- 🔜 Birthday and "eye test due" lists
- ⭐ Customer loyalty points / membership

## 3. Eye Test / Prescription Records
- ✅ Full prescription for Right and Left eye: Distance & Near, SPH, CYL, AXIS, VA
- ✅ PD, Addition, Vertex Distance, Refractive Index, Fitting Height, Lens, Shade, Frame
- ✅ Spectacle / Contact Lens / Sunglasses type, usage (Constant / Near / Distance)
- ✅ Visit-wise history: compare old and new power
- ✅ **One-click "copy last prescription"** for repeat customers
- ✅ Print prescription slip
- ⭐ Send prescription to customer on WhatsApp

## 4. Billing (Fast Checkout)
- ✅ **Everything on one screen**: customer → prescription → products → payment → print
- ✅ Product categories: Frames, Lenses, Contact Lenses, Solutions, Sunglasses, Miscellaneous
- ✅ Add products by **barcode scan** or quick search
- ✅ Automatic calculation of discount (% or ₹), GST, round-off, total and balance
- ✅ **Split payments**: Cash, Card, UPI (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm / BHIM), Cheque
- ✅ Advance payment & balance due tracking, collect remaining amount at delivery
- ✅ Delivery date and order status: Pending → In Lab → Ready → Delivered
- ✅ Print customer invoice and **workshop job card**
- ✅ Keyboard shortcuts for fast billing
- 🔜 Returns & exchange with credit note
- 🔜 EMI / installment plans
- ⭐ Send bill on WhatsApp / SMS as PDF

## 5. Inventory / Stock Management
- ✅ Product catalogue with brand, model, material, colour, shape, size, price, GST
- ✅ Separate stock count for each store
- ✅ Stock automatically reduced when a bill is made
- ✅ Quick stock entry for new purchases
- ✅ **Low-stock alerts**
- 🔜 Stock history (who added / sold / adjusted what and when)
- 🔜 Barcode label printing for frames
- 🔜 Supplier management and purchase orders
- 🔜 Stock valuation report

## 6. Dashboard
- ✅ Today's and this month's sales, per store or combined
- ✅ Pending deliveries and orders ready for pickup
- ✅ Total balance due from customers
- ✅ Low-stock items
- ✅ Sales trend chart and recent bills

## 7. Reports
- 🔜 Daily / monthly sales report (store-wise, staff-wise, category-wise)
- 🔜 GST report for filing
- 🔜 Payment mode summary (cash vs UPI vs card), useful for day-end closing
- 🔜 Top-selling brands and products
- 🔜 Export to Excel / PDF

## 8. Notifications ⭐
- ⭐ WhatsApp/SMS when spectacles are ready for pickup
- ⭐ Yearly eye-test reminder
- ⭐ Birthday / festival offers

## 9. Security & Reliability
- ✅ Secure login for every staff member
- ✅ Cloud database with automatic backups
- 🔜 Activity log: who created, edited or deleted a bill
- 🔜 Works on a slow internet connection; offline mode ⭐

---

## Why it's better than the current software
| Current software | EyeFit |
|---|---|
| Separate customer and billing screens | One screen for the complete sale |
| Many buttons (New / Add / Update / Save) | Auto-save and auto-calculation, fewer clicks |
| Single computer, single store | All 3 stores, from any device, anywhere |
| No business overview | Live dashboard for the owner |
| Old Windows-style interface | Clean, modern, easy to learn |
